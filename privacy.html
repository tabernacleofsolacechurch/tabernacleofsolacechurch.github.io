Privacy policy

How Tabernacle of Solace Church handles your personal information.

Last updated: 2026. This policy should be reviewed by the church's leadership and, ideally, a Nigerian legal adviser before the church relies on it.

Who we are

Tabernacle of Solace Church (TSC) is a church in Ibadan, Nigeria. You can contact us at .

What we collect and why

  • Membership and visitor forms: name, phone number, optional email, area, contact preference and your message. Used to welcome you and follow up, with your consent.
  • Programme registrations: name, phone, optional email, number of attendees and answers to programme questions. Used to organise the programme.
  • Prayer requests: your request, and optional name and contact details. Used only by authorised pastoral personnel to pray for and, if you ask, to contact you.
  • Testimonies: your testimony and name preference. Nothing is published without review and your permission.
  • Contact and partnership enquiries: the details you give us, used to reply.
  • Bible plan and lesson progress: stored only on your own device, not on our servers.

We try to collect only what we need. We do not sell your information and we do not use prayer requests for marketing.

Who can see it

Access is limited to authorised church personnel according to their role. Pastoral notes, prayer requests and follow-up records are restricted. We do not publish individual personal information or donations.

How long we keep it

  • Prayer requests: up to 12 months, then deleted or anonymised.
  • Visitor records: up to 12 months after your last contact.
  • Programme registrations: up to 24 months.
  • Membership records: while you are a member, and up to 24 months after you become inactive.
  • Contact enquiries: up to 12 months.

Your choices

You may ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by emailing us. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Security

We use access controls, secure connections and restricted permissions. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Third-party services

This website may load fonts from Google, embed YouTube videos, and link to WhatsApp, Telegram, social networks and Bible Gateway. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
