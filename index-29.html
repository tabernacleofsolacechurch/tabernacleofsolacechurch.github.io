Our vision

To be a tabernacle of solace for the nations: a place where the broken find comfort, the bound find freedom, and the weary find rest.

  • A generation of whole, Spirit-filled believers who carry God's presence into every home, workplace and community.
  • Families restored, minds renewed and destinies awakened by the power of God.
  • A church that is known across Ibadan, Nigeria and the nations for love, truth and deliverance.

Our mission

To restore broken souls and renew faith in Jesus Christ through the clear preaching of the Word, prophetic ministry, deliverance and loving discipleship.

  • Preach the gospel of Jesus Christ with clarity, boldness and compassion.
  • Lead people into freedom, healing and a living relationship with God.
  • Disciple believers to spiritual maturity, sound character and wise stewardship.
  • Show the love of Christ through care for the hurting, the poor and the lost.

The Lord hath sent me to bind up the brokenhearted, to proclaim liberty to the captives.

What we believe

Our faith rests on the Bible and on Jesus Christ. These are the truths we hold and teach.

The Bible
We believe the Bible is the inspired, trustworthy Word of God and the final authority for faith and life.2 Timothy 3:16
One God in three persons
We believe in one God who exists eternally as Father, Son and Holy Spirit. He is the Creator of all things and worthy of all worship.Matthew 28:19
Jesus Christ
We believe Jesus Christ is fully God and fully man. He was born of the virgin Mary, lived without sin, died on the cross for our sins, rose bodily from the dead, and ascended to the Father.1 Corinthians 15:3-4
Salvation by grace
We believe every person needs salvation, and that it is received by grace through faith in Jesus Christ, not by our own works.Ephesians 2:8-9
The Holy Spirit
We believe the Holy Spirit lives in every believer, empowers us to live for God, and gives spiritual gifts, including prophecy and healing, for the building up of the church.Acts 1:8, 1 Corinthians 12
Healing and deliverance
We believe Jesus has authority over sickness, bondage and every power of darkness, and that He still sets people free today.Luke 4:18
The church
We believe the church is the family of all who follow Christ. We gather to worship, to learn the Word, to pray, to share communion and baptism, and to serve one another.Acts 2:42
Christ's return and eternal life
We believe Jesus Christ will return in glory, and that all who trust in Him will live with Him forever.John 14:3
