About TSC

A place of restoration, spiritual growth and comfort in God's presence.

Who we are

Tabernacle of Solace Church (TSC) is a church in Ibadan, Nigeria, led by Apostle Oluwaseun Israel. Our identity centres on restoration, comfort, spiritual growth, prayer, the teaching of God's Word, discipleship, healing, deliverance and pastoral care.

Tabernacle
A sacred dwelling place, where God meets with His people.
Solace
Comfort and relief in a time of sorrow, pressure or loss.
Church
A family of believers who worship, grow and serve together.

Our story

Our story is just beginning. TSC is establishing its physical presence in Ibadan, Nigeria, and we are building a church family around the Word of God, prayer and love.

As the church grows, this page will record our journey. We will not claim facilities, branches or programmes that do not yet exist.

Our vision

To be a tabernacle of solace for the nations: a place where the broken find comfort, the bound find freedom, and the weary find rest.

Our mission

To restore broken souls and renew faith in Jesus Christ through the clear preaching of the Word, prophetic ministry, deliverance, and loving discipleship.

The Lord hath sent me to bind up the brokenhearted, to proclaim liberty to the captives.

Isaiah 61:1 (KJV)

Our core values

God's presence

Everything we do begins with prayer and a hunger for God.

Restoration

No one is too broken. God rebuilds lives, families and faith.

The Word

We teach the Bible clearly and live by it.

Love

We welcome people as they are and walk with them as they grow.

What we believe

Our faith rests on the Bible and on Jesus Christ. These are the truths we hold and teach.

The Bible
We believe the Bible is the inspired, trustworthy Word of God and the final authority for faith and life.2 Timothy 3:16
One God in three persons
We believe in one God who exists eternally as Father, Son and Holy Spirit. He is the Creator of all things and worthy of all worship.Matthew 28:19
Jesus Christ
We believe Jesus Christ is fully God and fully man. He was born of the virgin Mary, lived without sin, died on the cross for our sins, rose bodily from the dead, and ascended to the Father.1 Corinthians 15:3-4
Salvation by grace
We believe every person needs salvation, and that it is received by grace through faith in Jesus Christ, not by our own works.Ephesians 2:8-9
The Holy Spirit
We believe the Holy Spirit lives in every believer, empowers us to live for God, and gives spiritual gifts, including prophecy and healing, for the building up of the church.Acts 1:8, 1 Corinthians 12
Healing and deliverance
We believe Jesus has authority over sickness, bondage and every power of darkness, and that He still sets people free today.Luke 4:18
The church
We believe the church is the family of all who follow Christ. We gather to worship, to learn the Word, to pray, to share communion and baptism, and to serve one another.Acts 2:42
Christ's return and eternal life
We believe Jesus Christ will return in glory, and that all who trust in Him will live with Him forever.John 14:3

How we serve

Every part of our church life exists to restore people and help them stand strong in faith.

Prayer and prophetic ministry

Seek God together and receive a word for your season, in a setting that is led by Scripture and love.

Deliverance and restoration

Find freedom from what has held you back, and rebuild your life with God's help.

Discipleship and teaching

Grow in the Word step by step, from your first day of faith to mature, confident discipleship.

Financial wisdom

Learn to manage money, work and resources with biblical principles and a clear head.

Pastoral care

Someone to listen, pray with you and walk beside you through grief, pressure and hard decisions.

Apostle Oluwaseun Israel

Lead Pastor

Apostle Oluwaseun Israel is a prophetic minister, author and teacher with 17 years in ministry. He is the founder of the Break Free Tribe platform, where he has published 10 books and created 25 courses to help people grow in faith and live free.

His message is practical and rooted in Scripture. He leads Tabernacle of Solace Church with a heart for people who are weary, wounded or waiting on God.
